Weaving Stories in the Palm of a Hand

Melanau Terendak craftsmanship documented stage by stage, filmed entirely on a phone so the record could be made inside people's homes and workspaces.

Kampung Tanam, Mukah Research poster Mobile documentary

Long Akah Fort and The Lost City

A fort built in 1929 during the White Rajah era, left vulnerable to decay in the Baram interior. Recorded in 360 degrees with the people who remember it.

Long Akah, Baram Research poster Cinematic VR

Chinese Calligraphy

Digital archiving and storytelling around a practice thinning between generations, weaving the strokes of the brush together with the voices of its keepers.

Kuching Research poster Film and oral history

Pua Kumbu

The Iban ceremonial cloth, recognised as National Heritage in Malaysia in 2012, recorded as living practice rather than as a static object.

Sarawak Research poster Extended reality

Gawe ka Padi

The Salako harvest festival, celebrated again in June 2024 after the pandemic and filmed vertically because that is how the community already watches it. Awarded at the Nitiin Film Festival and SmartFone Flick Fest.
