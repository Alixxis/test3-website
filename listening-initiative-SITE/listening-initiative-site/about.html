The Listening Initiative

Storytelling as the interface between people and technology.

The Listening Initiative brings together researchers from Swinburne University of Technology Sarawak and Swinburne University of Technology Hawthorn to co-produce research projects with communities in Sarawak. The work uses extended reality, cinematic VR, mobile storytelling and photography, and it is made alongside Dayak, Melanau and local communities.

Each project documents a craft, a place or an occasion that is thinning between generations. The methods are deliberately portable: a phone, a 360-degree camera, a laptop, carried into a longhouse or up a river. Co-creation decentralises authorship, moves away from extractive research models, and aligns with UNESCO frameworks for safeguarding intangible cultural heritage.

The initiative is developed by Creative Media for Social Change at Swinburne Sarawak, bringing together the Centre for Transformative Media Technologies, the Swinburne Documentary Research Group, and the Centre for Innovative Society.
