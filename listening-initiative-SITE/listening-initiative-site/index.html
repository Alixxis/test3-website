Featured project

Weaving Stories in the Palm of a Hand

Melanau Terendak craftsmanship in Kampung Tanam, documented stage by stage and filmed entirely on a phone, so the record could be made inside people's homes without a crew standing between them and the camera.
Projects

Long Akah Fort and The Lost City

A fort built in 1929 during the White Rajah era, left vulnerable to decay in the Baram interior. Recorded in 360 degrees with the people who remember it, so it can be walked through remotely.

Long Akah, BaramCinematic VR

Chinese Calligraphy

Digital archiving and storytelling around a practice thinning between generations, weaving the strokes of the brush together with the voices of the people who keep it.

KuchingFilm and oral history

Pua Kumbu

The Iban ceremonial cloth, recognised as National Heritage in Malaysia in 2012, recorded as living practice rather than as a static object in a collection.

SarawakExtended reality

Gawe ka Padi

The Salako harvest festival, celebrated again in June 2024 after the pandemic and filmed vertically because that is the shape in which the community already watches and shares it.
