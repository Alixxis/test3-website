# The Listening Initiative

Static website for the Listening Initiative, a research programme of Swinburne
University of Technology Sarawak and Swinburne University of Technology Hawthorn.

Plain HTML and CSS, no build step. Every page loads `site.css` and `site.js`.

    index.html                         home
    projects.html                      all projects, filterable by method
    about.html                         about the initiative, people, exhibition
    documentary-research-group.html    the research group (draft, see below)
    weaving-stories-terendak.html      project 1
    long-akah-fort.html                project 2
    chinese-calligraphy.html           project 3
    pua-kumbu.html                     project 4
    gawe-ka-padi.html                  project 5
    404.html                           shown for unknown addresses
    site.css                           all styling, colours and typefaces at the top
    site.js                            reveal on scroll, project filter
    images/                            photographs
    posters/                           research poster PDFs (see posters/README.txt)
    .nojekyll                          required, tells GitHub Pages to serve files as-is

## Before publishing

The five research poster PDFs are not yet in `posters/`. Until they are added,
the "Research poster" links will show the 404 page. Filenames must match exactly,
including capitals; they are listed in `posters/README.txt`.

The Documentary Research Group page is a draft. Sections needing real content are
marked with orange placeholder boxes in the page itself.

Project text is drawn from the December poster material and has not been checked
by anyone who was in the field.
