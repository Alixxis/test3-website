About the group

Documentary as a research method, not only an output.

The Documentary Research Group brings together Swinburne researchers whose practice sits in documentary and non-fiction screen work. Its interests span mobile and smartphone filmmaking, immersive and 360-degree documentary, community co-creation, and the ethics of recording other people's lives and knowledge.

Members work across the Hawthorn campus and Swinburne Sarawak, and much of the group's recent field activity has run through the Listening Initiative. The group treats making as a form of enquiry: films, immersive works and photographic records are produced as non-traditional research outputs alongside written scholarship, and the production process itself is examined as method. In December 2024 the group convened the inaugural Swinburne Documentary and Non-Fiction Symposium.

What the group works on

Three running threads.

Mobile documentary

Smartphone filmmaking as a research instrument, including vertical and small-screen forms.

Immersive and 360

Cinematic VR and place-based storytelling for heritage sites and education.

Co-creation and ethics

Working with communities as co-authors rather than subjects, and what that demands of a research design.
